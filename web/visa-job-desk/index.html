How it works

Find jobs you can actually take, then apply with a tailored pack

Paste a US job posting. The desk checks what it says about visas, scores how well you fit, and drafts your cover letter, resume bullets and application-form answers. You always read everything and press Submit yourself.

Five steps

  1. Upload your CVA PDF or Word file. Claude fills in your profile; you answer the visa questions. About 5 minutes, once. Job hunting as a couple? Use "+ Add person" at the top: each person gets their own profile and tracker.
  2. Find a jobOn LinkedIn, Indeed or a company careers page. Copy the full posting text.
  3. Check itPaste it under "Check a job". The visa stamp appears instantly: PASS, FLAG, UNVERIFIED or FAIL.
  4. Score and draftPress "Score my fit", then draft the cover letter, resume bullets and recruiter note. Edit anything that isn't you.
  5. Autofill, apply and trackUse the Autofill bookmark on the company's application form, check it, submit it yourself, then save the job to the tracker.

What the visa stamps mean

PASS
Says sponsorship is available, or you don't need it
FLAG
Says no sponsorship, but it may only mean H-1B. Ask the recruiter.
UNVERIFIED
Doesn't mention visas. Check before you spend time on it.
FAIL
Citizens only, clearance required, or "no sponsorship" when you chose to drop those

Your data

Your profile and tracker are private to you. Scoring and drafting use your own Claude account, and the first time you ask, Claude asks your permission.

Visa guidance here is general, not legal advice. Confirm your route with an immigration attorney.

    Autofill

    Fill the company's application form in one click

    1. Drag the teal button below onto your browser's bookmarks bar. To show the bar, press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac).
    2. Open the job's application form on the company's own site (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and most others).
    3. Click the bookmark. It fills your contact details, education, work-authorization answers and the cover letter drafted above, and outlines each field it filled.
    4. Check every answer, attach your resume file, and press Submit yourself.
    Autofill

    Can't drag it?

    Press "Copy the code instead". In your browser, add a new bookmark, name it "Autofill", and paste the code into the URL (address) box. Re-copy it after you change your profile or draft a new cover letter.
